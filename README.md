# Café Calle Ocho – Ordering Management System

**Course:** EGN475  
**Assignment:** Week 1 – Assignment 2  
**Project Option:** Option A  
**Business:** Café Calle Ocho – Little Havana, Miami, Florida

## 1. Project Overview

Café Calle Ocho is a small Cuban café located in Little Havana. This project focuses on developing a web-based ordering management system that makes it easier for customers to place orders and for kitchen employees to manage their preparation.

The application will provide a digital menu, an ordering system, and a kitchen dashboard where employees can track orders from the moment they are received until they are picked up.

## 2. Problem Statement

Café Calle Ocho needs a better way to organize customer orders and coordinate the preparation process between customers and kitchen staff. A manual or disorganized ordering process can cause confusion, delays, and uncertainty about order status. Our prototype will allow customers to browse a digital menu and place orders while kitchen employees manage orders through a dashboard with clear status updates. The goal is to make ordering easier for customers and help employees handle daily orders more efficiently.

## 3. Target Users (Personas)

### Persona 1: Marta Rodriguez – Customer

- **Role:** Regular café customer.
- **Main Goals:** Browse the menu, place orders quickly, and know when orders are ready for pickup.
- **Frustrations:** Waiting without knowing order status and having to ask employees for updates.
- **Technology Comfort:** High. Regularly uses websites and mobile applications.
- **Quote:** "I just want to order my coffee, know when it's ready, and pick it up without wasting time."

### Persona 2: Carlos Martinez – Kitchen Employee

- **Role:** Kitchen employee responsible for preparing orders.
- **Main Goals:** View incoming orders, understand customer requests, and update order statuses.
- **Frustrations:** Managing multiple orders, receiving verbal instructions, and keeping track of orders during busy hours.
- **Technology Comfort:** Moderate. Comfortable with basic computer applications and dashboards.
- **Quote:** "When we have several orders coming in, I need everything organized so I can focus on preparing the food and drinks."

## 4. Must-Have Features

The initial prototype will include:

1. **Digital Menu:** Customers can view available drinks and food items with descriptions and prices.
2. **Order Placement:** Customers can select items and submit an order.
3. **Order Confirmation:** Customers receive an on-screen confirmation with their order information.
4. **Kitchen Order Board:** Employees can view incoming orders in an organized dashboard.
5. **Order Status Management:** Employees can update orders through received, preparing, ready, and picked up.
6. **Order History:** Previously placed orders can be reviewed.
7. **Menu Administration:** Authorized staff can add, edit, and remove menu items.

## 5. Out-of-Scope Features

The following features are not included in the initial prototype:

1. **Online Payments:** No payment processing through the application.
2. **SMS and Email Notifications:** No automatic notifications when orders are ready.
3. **Mobile Application:** The initial project will be a web application, not a dedicated iOS or Android app.
4. **Loyalty Program:** No customer points, rewards, or membership system.
5. **Delivery Services:** The application will focus on customer pickup.
6. **Advanced Analytics:** No advanced financial reports or sales predictions.

## 6. Project Goals

The main goals of this project are:

- Make the ordering process easier for customers.
- Improve communication between customers and kitchen staff.
- Help employees organize and track incoming orders.
- Reduce confusion during busy business hours.
- Create a simple and user-friendly ordering management system.

## 7. Future Development

Future versions could include online payments, automatic order notifications, customer loyalty programs, and delivery options. These features may be considered after the initial prototype is completed and evaluated.
